# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Kangaroo welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Show empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent, and reasonable requests to stop an interaction.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualised attention, trolling,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information
without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository maintainers or,
when safety or confidentiality is relevant, through GitHub's private reporting
facilities. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as practical,
investigate fairly, and may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation according
to the severity and pattern of behaviour.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project. Enforcement decisions may be appealed privately to a maintainer
who was not involved in the original decision when one is available.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
